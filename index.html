Click Before Frames

A predictive input buffer for Geometry Dash. Clicked a little too early? It waits for the moment your click actually works.

Get it How it works

Geode mod · Geometry Dash 2.2081 · Windows

Features

Every click is tested ahead of time with a hidden copy of your player that runs the game's own physics. If clicking now would crash and clicking a few frames later wouldn't, your click is held until then.

Every game mode

Cube, ship, ball, UFO, wave, robot, spider and swing, each with its own rules for what counts as a safe input.

Orbs, pads and portals

Clicks are moved onto orbs, pads are followed, and predictions keep going through mode, teleport, dual and solo portals.

Moving objects and triggers

Spikes and blocks moved by move, rotate, toggle and spawn triggers are predicted where they will be, not where they are now.

Smart robot and spider

Robot hold lengths and spider flips are chosen so the whole jump lands safely, not just the first frames.

Level memory

Learns which timings work at each spot of a level over your runs, and gives extra time to the spots where you keep dying.

Built for high refresh rates

Works with Click Between Frames and custom tick rates. A time budget keeps every decision fast, and long searches carry on over the next frames instead of stuttering.

Your choice of stage

From 0.5 to 8 frames of buffer or a custom amount, switched with one key. Low latency mode keeps it to a single frame.

Training mode and tools

Hints instead of saves, a path view, a death heatmap and a live log that explains every decision.

How it works

No macros, no recorded inputs. It only ever uses clicks you made, and it can only make them later, never earlier.

You click

The mod looks at where you are right now: speed, mode, gravity, size and what's ahead of you.

A hidden ghost tries it

An invisible copy of your player runs the next second of the level, once for clicking now and again for each later timing.

Your click lands when it works

If clicking now survives, nothing changes. If a slightly later click survives and clicking now doesn't, your click is held for exactly that long.

FAQ

What do I need?

Geometry Dash 2.2081 on Windows with the Geode mod loader installed. You install the mod file you get after buying, start the game and enter your license key once.

Does it need internet?

Only to activate your key the first time and for a short check every few days. It keeps working offline for a few days between checks.

Can I use it on more than one PC?

One license is for one PC. If you get a new PC, email us and we'll move your license over.

Does it play for me?

No. It never makes a click you didn't make. It only holds an early click until the moment it works. Auto-click and auto-release helpers are off by default and only for practice.

Will it make me lag?

Each decision has a strict time limit (a few milliseconds by default), and a performance mode is included for slower PCs.

Fair use: Click Before Frames changes when your inputs reach the game. Use it for practice and your own enjoyment. Don't use it for runs you submit to leaderboards, the Demon List or verification, or anywhere else that doesn't allow input-assist mods.
